Hi, I'm Zhen. I am a long-term investor. I have kept a ledger since I was a teenager.    And for the last two years I've written down every single trade by hand.

My ledger can tell me exactly how much I lost. Down to the cent.     It has never told me why I lost it.  I cannot change what I lost. I can only change what I do next.

And it is not just me. Between 1991 and 1996, two researchers studied sixty-six thousand American households.  
The ones who traded the most earned eleven point four percent a year. The market earned seventeen point nine. That gap is not about picking bad stocks. It is about behaviour.

So I went looking for a tool.  The open-source ones have no AI.    The AI ones only follow the money.  
The ones that look at psychology sit in the cloud, expensive, and ask me to fill in a form.     I don't want to fill in a form.

So I stopped looking. I built my own.  My ledger already knows when every trade happened, and what the price was doing then.  
Take one trade. I bought at the top of a day that went up fourteen percent.  
The trade itself is the data.  I never had to tell anyone how I felt.

**But what if I do say something?**   Every trade in my ledger can also keep the sentence I wrote at that moment. 
I built that field before I ever had this idea.So I wrote: the fundamentals changed, I am here for the long term.  
And my ledger says: you bought at the top of a fourteen percent day.
**One of those two sentences is a lie. And it is not the ledger.**
And the trades where I wrote nothing? Those are the ones I did not want to explain.

A fixed rule can only catch the excuses I already thought of.  **And I am creative. I always find a new one.**  
No rule written in advance can keep up with me. But AI can.

Wealth does not come from being right once.  It comes from not making the same mistake twice.  
And you cannot stop a mistake if you do not remember it.  Every tool today tells you what happened to your money.  
I am building the one that tells you what happened to you.
